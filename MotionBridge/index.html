ONE APP, MANY WAYS TO CONTROL

A little remote.
A lot of possibilities.

From the sofa to the front of the room, choose the control that fits the moment.

01 / MOTION
⌖

Point naturally

Move your phone to guide a laser pointer or the mouse. Adjust sensitivity, smoothness and update rate to suit you.

02 / TOUCH
↗

A touchpad in your pocket

Move, click, scroll and drag with your fingers—no pointing or calibration needed.

03 / PRESENT
▱

Own the room

Move through slides, switch apps and draw on screen while you present.

04 / MEDIA
▶

Stay in control

Use the remote keyboard and control supported media. On iPhone, you can also preview your computer screen.

MADE FOR REAL MOMENTS

Your computer, within reach.

A friction-free way to stay in control when your computer is across the room.

Present with confidence

Move through slides and point to the details while you speak, without standing next to your computer.

Relax and watch

Pause a video, change the volume or navigate your computer from the sofa.

Take control anywhere in the room

Use your phone as a touchpad or keyboard when your computer is connected to a larger screen.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN

Ready when you are.

One phone, one computer, and a connection. Save your devices to reconnect without entering a key each time.

01

Open both apps

Start MotionBridge on your iPhone or Android phone and on your Mac or Windows PC.

02

Connect securely

Connect over a reachable local network and pair with your computer.

03

Make your point

Choose touchpad, pointer or slides. Set the center when using motion control.

A PROJECT IN PROGRESS

Made to explore.
Built to be useful.

MotionBridge is a university project in active development. iPhone and Android apps connect to macOS and Windows companions. Features and connection options vary by platform.

Two-phone UWB and AR pointer modes are experimental on Mac. A public download is not available here yet.
